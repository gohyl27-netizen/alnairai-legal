AlnairAI Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 1 October 2026

AlnairAI is an internal company AI assistant operated by Alnair Photonics for authorized users.

How to request deletion

If you want to request deletion of personal information associated with your AlnairAI account or WhatsApp access, contact Alnair Photonics through the official company contact channel or the contact email listed for the AlnairAI Meta application.

Please include enough information to identify the relevant AlnairAI account or WhatsApp number. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other security credentials.

What may be deleted

Depending on the request and applicable company or legal requirements, deletion may include account-linked personal information, WhatsApp number mappings, and conversation history associated with the requesting user.

Retention exceptions

Some information may need to be retained where required for security, legal, audit, backup, or legitimate business-record purposes. Where deletion cannot be completed immediately or fully, the request will be reviewed and handled according to applicable requirements and company policy.

Confirmation

After a valid request is reviewed, Alnair Photonics may confirm completion or provide information about any data that must be retained.
