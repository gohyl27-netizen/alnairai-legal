AlnairAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

AlnairAI is an internal company AI assistant operated by Alnair Photonics for authorized employees and approved users. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users access AlnairAI, including through WhatsApp.

1. Information we may process

Depending on how AlnairAI is used, the system may process information such as a user's name, work account, phone number used for WhatsApp access, messages sent to AlnairAI, and company documents or information that authorized users choose to make available to the system.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized users, answer work-related questions, search approved company knowledge, provide summaries or suggestions, maintain user conversation history, and operate and secure the AlnairAI service.

3. WhatsApp integration

When an authorized user communicates with AlnairAI through WhatsApp, messages are transmitted through the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform before reaching AlnairAI. The user's WhatsApp phone number may be mapped to an authorized AlnairAI account so the system can confirm that the sender is permitted to use the service.

4. Company data and storage

AlnairAI is designed so that company files, indexed knowledge, account information, and application data are primarily stored on company-controlled systems. Third-party services used for message transport or connectivity may process limited technical information required to deliver those services.

5. Sharing of information

AlnairAI does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only where necessary to operate the service, such as Meta for WhatsApp message delivery, or where required by law.

6. Access and security

Access to AlnairAI is limited to authorized users. Reasonable security measures are used to protect accounts, company information, and system access. Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure.

7. Data retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for business operations, security, record keeping, or legal requirements. Retention may vary depending on the type of data and company policy.

8. Data access or deletion requests

Authorized users may contact Alnair Photonics to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information where applicable. Requests will be reviewed in accordance with company policy and applicable law.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AlnairAI features, integrations, or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published at this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact Alnair Photonics using the contact email listed in the AlnairAI Meta application or the company's official contact channel.
